Overview

Dave Biehl Art runs davebiehlart.com to share Dave Biehl's artwork, events, press, and gallery hours. The site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and nothing for sale.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The site doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and we don't build profiles of visitors.

Stored on your device

If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Services that load content

To show some pages, your browser loads content directly from these services. Like any website, they can see your IP address and basic browser details, and they handle them under their own privacy policies:

Articles on the Media page link to news sites. Those sites see your visit only if you follow the link.

Email and phone

If you email or call Dave, your message and contact details are used only to reply to you. They are never sold or shared.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
